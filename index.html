Pergola math

A pergola sketched on the back of a receipt ends up a rafter short, a post too far apart, or throwing shade on the wrong half of the terrace. Pergola math turns the span and a labeled pitch into the rafter count, turns the run and a labeled bay into the bays and posts, and turns the footprint and a labeled coverage into the shade it throws. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Rafter plan - the span at a labeled pitch becomes the rafter count, both ends included, and the extra spacing.
  • Post plan - the run at a labeled bay becomes the bay count and the post count.
  • Shade plan - the footprint at a labeled coverage becomes the square meters of shade.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 60 cm rafter pitch, a 300 cm post bay, 50% shade coverage - all illustrative defaults you can change. Real pergolas carry wind, wisteria and local rules about footings and heights. This is planning arithmetic, not a structural design: measure the real site and let the timber span tables and local codes govern.
